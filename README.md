Entry for [make.fun's "Make the best flight game" bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/best-flight-game-20261126-0057).

A bush flying game: take off from a mountain valley runway, thread a series
of glowing rings over fields, forest, a lake and high ridges, then bring the
Skylark home and land on runway 27 before the fuel runs out. Three sorties
with different routes, winds and light: First Solo, Ridge Courier and Storm
Return. Throttle, pitch, roll and yaw answer at every speed, the fuel gauge
and the hills turn each sortie into decisions, and a gentle touchdown with
the gear down earns a landing bonus while a hard one ends the flight.

Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make the best flight game" bounty, $500 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome.

## Play

- Arrow keys fly the stick: Up and Down pitch, Left and Right roll.
- W and S work the throttle, A and D add rudder yaw.
- Hold the mouse button and move it to steer with the mouse; the wheel works the throttle.
- G lowers or raises the landing gear. P pauses. R restarts the sortie.
- 1, 2 or 3 pick a mission on the title screen; Enter starts it.
- Fly every ring, then land on the runway: slow, gentle, gear down.
